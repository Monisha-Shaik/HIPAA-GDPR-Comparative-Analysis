# HIPAA–GDPR Comparative Analysis

**Health Data Privacy and Security Under HIPAA and GDPR: A Comparative Analysis of Current and Proposed Requirements**

Researcher: Monisha Shaik · Indiana University · Fall 2026 proposal

## Research overview

This proposed study compares how HIPAA and GDPR protect the privacy and security of health-related information and considers the practical implications for healthcare organizations, including organizations subject to both frameworks.

**Primary research question:** How do HIPAA and GDPR differ in their approaches to protecting the privacy and security of health-related data?

The [main research proposal](01_Research_Proposal/HIPAA_GDPR_Research_Proposal.docx) is the source for this overview. The research scope covers developments from 2020 through October 31, 2026, with earlier material used to establish a baseline. The proposal includes consideration of the January 2025 HIPAA Security Rule modernization proposal and the November 2025 Digital Omnibus package. These are descriptions of the proposal's scope, not an independent verification of their present legal status.

## Planned methodology and outputs

The study proposes qualitative comparative regulatory analysis using structured document analysis of primary regulatory texts, government guidance, and authoritative secondary literature. It anticipates a corpus of approximately 40–60 documents; no interviews, surveys, or organizational data collection are planned.

The Excel comparison matrix will cover eight categories:

1. Regulatory scope
2. Privacy requirements
3. Security safeguards
4. Risk-management obligations
5. Third-party responsibilities
6. Breach notification
7. Enforcement
8. Penalties

For each category, the matrix will record HIPAA and GDPR requirements, governing primary sources, alignment or divergence, and practical implications. Legal status and the date of verification will be recorded so proposed provisions are distinguished from requirements in force and from vacated or withdrawn provisions.

The proposal's deliverable is a written comparative analysis organized by these categories, with the completed comparison matrix as an appendix and APA 7 citations.

Three working expectations will be evaluated, not treated as findings: closer convergence on technical safeguards; sharper divergence in scope and individual rights; and breach-notification differences concentrated in timing and reporting thresholds.
