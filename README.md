# HIPAA–GDPR Comparative Analysis

**Health Data Privacy and Security Under HIPAA and GDPR: A Comparative Analysis of Current and Proposed Requirements**

Monisha Shaik · Indiana University · Fall 2026

## Research overview

This research examines how HIPAA and GDPR allocate privacy and security responsibilities for health-data processing. It compares specific obligations and their conditions to explain where organizations subject to both frameworks can use shared governance activities and where separate legal decisions are necessary.

**Research question:** How do HIPAA and GDPR allocate privacy and security responsibilities for health-data processing, and what do their similarities and differences imply for organizations subject to both frameworks?

## Scope and approach

The legal baseline is **October 31, 2026**. The study examines regulatory developments from 2020 through that date, using earlier legal materials where necessary to establish the baseline. Proposed reforms and developments after the cutoff are distinguished from requirements applicable at the cutoff.

The study uses qualitative comparative regulatory document analysis, guided by the READ approach: Ready materials, Extract data, Analyse data and Distil findings. The comparison examines responsible actors, scope, triggers, actions, recipients, timing, exceptions and legal status, with supporting provisions and source references. The analysis concerns legal obligations and governance implications; it does not measure organizational compliance or patient outcomes.

## Comparison domains

The matrix organizes the analysis into eight domains:

1. Regulatory scope
2. Privacy requirements
3. Security safeguards
4. Risk management
5. Third-party responsibilities
6. Breach notification
7. Enforcement
8. Penalties

For each topic, it records HIPAA and GDPR requirements, supporting provisions, source identifiers, legal status, relevant proposed changes, similarities and differences, and practical implications.
