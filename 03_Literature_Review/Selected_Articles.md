# Selected Articles

Six scholarly articles selected for the HIPAA–GDPR literature review. They provide context and evidence on how privacy rules are interpreted and applied. The comparison matrix cites official regulatory sources for legal requirements.

| Article | Contribution to the project |
| --- | --- |
| Becker, R., Chokoshvili, D., Comandé, G., Dove, E. S., Hall, A., Mitchell, C., Molnár-Gábor, F., Nicolás, P., Tervo, S., & Thorogood, A. (2023). Secondary use of personal health data: When is it “further processing” under the GDPR, and what are the implications for data controllers? *European Journal of Health Law, 30*(2), 129–157. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1163/15718093-bja10094) | Explains how the original purpose of collection affects subsequent use of health data under GDPR. |
| Conduah, A. K., Ofoe, S., & Siaw-Marfo, D. (2025). Data privacy in healthcare: Global challenges and solutions. *DIGITAL HEALTH, 11*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/20552076251343959) | Provides international context on healthcare privacy and the role of organizational and technical practices. |
| Cornejo, G. M. (2026). Third-party risk in U.S. health care ransomware incidents: Business associate involvement and breach size. *Health and Technology, 16*, 609–620. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12553-025-01036-9) | Uses reported U.S. ransomware incidents to examine business associate involvement and breach size. |
| Jirsa, D., & Horňáková, A. (2026). General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) enforcement in European healthcare: A comparative analysis of publicly reported enforcement decisions, 2018–2023. *Archives of Public Health*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13690-026-02081-0) | Examines publicly reported decisions to show how GDPR enforcement varies across European healthcare settings. |
| Tschider, C., Corrales Compagnucci, M., & Minssen, T. (2024). The new EU-US data protection framework’s implications for healthcare. *Journal of Law and the Biosciences, 11*(2), lsae022. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/jlb/lsae022) | Examines cross-border transfer options and their implications for healthcare organizations working across the U.S. and EU. |
| Zuiderveen Borgesius, F., Asghari, H., Bangma, N., & Hoepman, J.-H. (2023). The GDPR’s rules on data breaches: Analysing their rationales and effects. *SCRIPTed, 20*(2). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.2218/scrip.20.2.2023.8979) | Explains what GDPR breach-notification rules aim to achieve and where notification has practical limits. |
