# Research Paper

**Health Data Privacy and Security Under HIPAA and GDPR: A Comparative Analysis of Applicable Requirements and Selected Reforms**

Monisha Shaik · Indiana University Indianapolis

- [Word manuscript](HIPAA_GDPR_Research_Paper_Final_APA7.docx)
- [PDF manuscript](HIPAA_GDPR_Research_Paper_Final_APA7.pdf)

The APA 7 manuscript contains 52 pages, two figures, and one table. It examines 46 comparison topics across eight domains using an October 31, 2026 legal baseline. Proposed reforms, deferred application, and post-cutoff developments are distinguished from the baseline.

This version is available for faculty review; it has not undergone independent legal review or journal peer review.
